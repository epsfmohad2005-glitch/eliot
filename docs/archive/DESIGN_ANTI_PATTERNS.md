# Design Anti-Patterns (TSA-1)

## Banned AI Slop & Aesthetic Defects

### 1. Color & FX Anti-Patterns
* 🚫 **Purple-to-Blue Gradients**: Banned across all backgrounds and hero cards.
* 🚫 **Cyan Text on Dark Backgrounds**: Low contrast and visual noise.
* 🚫 **Uncalibrated Glassmorphism**: Blurry frosted glass cards over busy canvas images.
* 🚫 **Glowing Drop Shadows**: Radial colored glow boxes under standard cards.

### 2. Layout & Typography Anti-Patterns
* 🚫 **Nested Cards**: Placing cards inside cards. Use whitespace dividers instead.
* 🚫 **Hero Eyebrows**: Tiny uppercase tracked-out text labels above every heading.
* 🚫 **Wrapped Pill Text**: Text breaking onto two lines inside buttons or badges.
* 🚫 **Side-Tab Accent Borders**: Thick 4px left-border stripes on cards with rounded corners.
