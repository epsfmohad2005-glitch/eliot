# Eliot Visual Identity Bible (TSA-1)

## Executive Vision
Eliot Personal Learning & Knowledge Operating System (PLOS) is designed to operate as a high-precision intellectual workspace. The visual identity of Eliot reflects quiet focus, computational clarity, and cognitive endurance. It replaces artificial noise and generic SaaS aesthetics with mathematical spatial discipline, purposeful visual density, and high-contrast ergonomics.

---

## Brand Principles

### 1. Computational Clarity
* Interfaces present data without decorative clutter.
* Geometry is sharp, structural, and predictable.
* Whitespace functions as an active organizing element, not empty space.

### 2. High-Contrast Ergonomics
* High legibility in both light and dark operational modes.
* Strict adherence to WCAG AAA/AA contrast standards.
* Luminescence differences between canvas and cards are mathematically bounded ($<7\%$ light mode, $<12\%$ dark mode).

### 3. Purposeful Density
* Visual layouts adapt dynamically between dense analytical displays (knowledge graphs, telemetry) and spacious focus viewports (flashcard review, reflection writing).
* Typography scales mathematically with step ratios calibrated for dense data structures.

---

## Visual Philosophy
Eliot rejects generic AI templates ("AI Slop"), artificial glow effects, hero marketing headers, and uncalibrated gradients. Every visual element—from border widths to motion curves—is defined by mathematical constraints and semantic domain contracts.
